<h2>Getting started</h2>
<h3>Finding something to cook</h3>
<p>The <strong>Cook</strong> tab shows all 22 recipes, with a dish of the day at the top. Filter by country using the row of chips. Tap any dish to open it.</p>
<h3>Reading the flavor compass</h3>
<p>Every recipe is scored 0&ndash;5 on the five tastes: salty, heat, sour, umami and sweet. It tells you what a dish is going to taste like before you commit to cooking it.</p>
<h3>Changing servings and units</h3>
<p>Inside a recipe, use the &minus; and + buttons to set how many people you are feeding. Every ingredient <em>and</em> every quantity written into the instructions rescales instantly. The g/ml &harr; oz/cup switch beside it converts between metric and US units, and your choice is remembered.</p>
<h3>Cook mode</h3>
<p>Tap <strong>Cook now</strong> for large-type, step-by-step guidance. Steps that need timing have a timer built in &mdash; tap play to start it, and Timpla chimes and vibrates when time is up. Your screen stays awake for as long as you are in cook mode.</p>
<h3>The Timpla Doctor</h3>
<p>Under the <strong>Mine</strong> tab, open the Timpla Doctor when a dish has gone wrong. Choose what you are cooking (soup, stir-fry, curry, fried, braise) and what the problem is (too salty, flat, too sour, too sweet, too spicy, fishy, bitter) for technique that actually fixes it.</p>
<h3>Cooking from what you have</h3>
<p>In the <strong>Search</strong> tab, tap the ingredients that are in your kitchen. Timpla ranks every dish you can make and shows what is missing from the ones you cannot.</p>
<h3>The grocery list</h3>
<p>Tap the basket inside any recipe to add its ingredients. Ingredients shared between recipes are added together automatically, grouped by aisle, and scaled to the servings you picked. Tap an item to check it off.</p>

<h2>Frequently asked</h2>
<h3>Is Timpla free?</h3>
<p>Yes. Every feature is included, there are no in-app purchases, and there are no ads.</p>
<h3>Do I need an account or internet?</h3>
<p>Neither. Timpla works fully offline and has no sign-up at all.</p>
<h3>Where is my data stored? Can I move it to a new phone?</h3>
<p>Everything you save lives on your device only. It is included in your phone&rsquo;s regular backups (iPhone backups, or Android backup to your Google account), so restoring a backup to a new phone of the same kind brings your saved recipes, cook log and grocery list with it. There is no cloud sync between devices, and data does not move between iPhone and Android.</p>
<h3>Will you add more recipes?</h3>
<p>Yes &mdash; more dishes and regions are planned. If there is a dish you want, email it in; requests genuinely shape what gets added next.</p>
<h3>I found a mistake in a recipe.</h3>
<p>Please tell me. Email the dish name and what is wrong and I will correct it in the next update.</p>
<h3>How do I delete my data?</h3>
<p>Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Nothing of yours exists anywhere else.</p>

<h2>Refunds</h2>
<p>Timpla is free, so there is nothing to refund. If you ever purchase anything from Apple or Google by mistake, refunds are handled by the store: Apple at <a href="https://reportaproblem.apple.com">reportaproblem.apple.com</a>, Google Play at <a href="https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637">support.google.com/googleplay</a>.</p>

<h2>Requirements</h2>
<p>iPhone running iOS 17.0 or later, or an Android phone running Android 8.0 or later.</p>
